SHORT DESCRIPTION
A co-op horror spot-the-difference game for 2-4 players. You're in the same room,
but you might not see the same thing. Talk, compare, and radio in a single report.
Get it wrong, and you all start over.

ABOUT THE GAME
Nothing to Report is an online co-op horror game for 2-4 players. In anomaly games
like The Exit 8, you walk alone and rely on your memory to spot what changed.
Here, you find the difference by talking to your friends: everyone stands in the
same room, but each player's map may be different.

You see the poster on the wall; the person next to you doesn't. The number you call
a 6 is a 9 to them. Or everything is exactly the same. Only one rule: no looking at
each other's screens. Talk, describe, ask.

Every level ends with a single report. If you saw a difference, choose Report. If
everything matches, choose Nothing to report. One of you makes the call; get it
wrong and you all go back to level one.

There was a rift in spacetime. The reflections of our world, the ones that should
be identical, are changing, and your team has been sent in: a flashlight in your
hand, a radio in your ear. The operator on the radio starts out calm. She doesn't
stay that way.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- You find the difference by talking, not by memory. Each player may see a
  different version of the same room.
- One report per level. A single mistake resets the run for everyone.
- Everyone's sanity is their own. As it drops, scares come more often. Close your
  eyes to protect your mind, but then all you can do is listen.
- In-game proximity voice chat. Move apart and your voice goes over the radio, and
  the farther you are, the more it cuts out.
- A story and a fully voiced radio operator. English and Turkish voice-over,
  interface and subtitles in 12 languages.
